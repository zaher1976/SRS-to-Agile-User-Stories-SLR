## Bibliographic records

- Imported records: Scopus 93; IEEE Xplore 28; ScienceDirect citation collection 86; total **207**.

- Unique bibliographic *candidates*: **181**. This is **not** the number of included or critically appraised publications.
- The local audit index records 93 Scopus-, 7 IEEE-, and 81 ScienceDirect-origin records after deduplication. These numbers describe provenance **after** duplicate removal, not initial retrieval.
- The native full ScienceDirect search hit count and some search execution details are not independently verified in the archived audit.

## Evidence and framework

- Evidence roles: E1 direct story generation from written requirements; E2 adjacent tasks; E3 evaluation and traceability; E4 contextual literature.
- The source-grounded framework proposes obligation coverage, claim-source support, bidirectional traceability, story quality, acceptance-test readiness, run-to-run consistency, and human correction effort.
- **No empirical validation is claimed.** A basic CSV integrity script does not test the proposed evaluation framework.
- Do not automatically label every bibliographic candidate as an included study or assign an E1–E4 category without documented appraisal.

## Author-confirmed review procedure (11 November 2026)

Both authors, Zaher Fadhil Raham and Sura Salah, confirm that they selected and critically assessed the literature jointly by discussion and agreement. They did **not** conduct independent initial screening or independent ratings. No inter-rater agreement statistic (for example, Cohen's kappa) was calculated. This supersedes the previous single-reviewer description in the repository README; it is consistent with MJES manuscript v7 Section 3.1. This author confirmation does not itself validate individual screening records.
